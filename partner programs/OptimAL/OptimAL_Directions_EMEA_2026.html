Target audience

Who OptimAL is for

OptimAL is built for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central partners who own and maintain AL code — and who want to move from reactive firefighting to proactive performance management. One program, two tracks: you choose the one that fits your business.

ISV track

Independent Software Vendors

You ship Business Central apps to many customers — often with years (or decades) of legacy code.

  • Code scan of your solution
  • Use case review and recommendations
  • Learnings and measurable performance gains
VAR track

Resellers & implementation partners

You build customer-specific extensions and integrations and need them to scale.

  • Co-development of 2 use cases
  • Learning and code review
  • Measurement of the impact

You're a great fit if…

  • Your customers are feeling slow pages, long batch jobs or timeouts — or you want to get ahead of it.
  • You have a technical team (developers, architects, tech leads) who can commit time during the 10 weeks.
  • You have leadership backing to turn learnings into a prioritized backlog — and act on it.
  • You want to adopt agentic engineering in a safe, guided way, starting with performance.

Typical anti-patterns OptimAL uncovers

Thousands of these hide across decades of legacy code:

  • IsEmpty vs. Count
  • Missing SetLoadFields
  • FindFirst in loops
  • CalcFields in loops
  • Summing in loops instead of CalcSums
  • Unbuffered inserts
  • Missing bulk operations
  • Nested loops
  • N+1 lookups
  • Poor index usage
